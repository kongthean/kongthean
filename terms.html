Terms of Use · Thean Post

Thean · kongthean.com/post · Version 1 · Last updated 15 November 2026

Thean Post is an app by our team at Thean (kongthean.com) that helps you post videos and photos to your own Facebook Page and, with a plan, to Instagram and Telegram. By creating an account or using the app, you agree to these terms. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

1. Your account

  • Give true information (name, email, Facebook profile link) and keep your password safe.
  • One account is for one person or one business. Do not share or sell your account.
  • You must be at least 18 years old, or have permission from a parent or guardian.

2. Content you may post

  • Post only videos and photos that you own, or that the owner clearly allowed you to use. Writing the owner's name or username (credit) is not permission.
  • Respect the work of creators. If an owner asks you to remove their content, remove it.
  • Follow the rules of Facebook, Instagram, Telegram and TikTok. They can remove your posts or limit your Page, and we cannot change their decisions.

3. Content that is not allowed

  • Sexual content, nudity or pornography of any kind.
  • Any sexual content involving children. This is strictly forbidden and will be reported to the authorities.
  • Violence, threats, cruelty to people or animals, or content that encourages harm.
  • Hate speech, bullying, or content that insults people for their nation, religion, race, gender or disability.
  • Scams, fake news, gambling, drugs, weapons, or anything else that is illegal in the Kingdom of Cambodia.
  • Content that disrespects the Nation, Religion and King, or that harms the privacy of other people.
  • Spam, or posting other people's content as your own to cheat followers.

4. Plans and payments

  • New accounts get a number of free posts to Facebook. Facebook Story, Instagram and Telegram come with a paid plan.
  • You pay with KHQR and upload your receipt. Our system checks the receipt and turns the plan on; our team may check it again.
  • Sending a fake, edited or reused receipt is fraud. We will stop the plan, pause or close the account, and may report it.
  • Prices can change. A change does not affect a plan you have already paid for.
  • If a payment problem happens, contact us in the app chat and we will help.

5. Records we keep

To run the app and keep it safe, we keep your account details (name, email, phone, Facebook link), your activity history (posts made with the app: time, places, source link and caption), payments and receipts, and your chats with our team. See our Privacy policy.

6. When rules are broken

  • We can remove access, pause or close an account at any time if we find a violation of these terms, a fake payment, or misuse of the app.
  • If we find a violation, we may use the account information and activity history described above to investigate, and we may share them with the competent authorities to handle the matter under the laws of the Kingdom of Cambodia.
  • You are fully responsible for everything you post with this app.

7. The service

We work hard to keep the app running, but we cannot promise it will always work without breaks. Facebook, Instagram, Telegram and TikTok are separate companies; when they change or block something, some features may stop working. We are not responsible for losses caused by these services or by content you post.

8. Changes and law

We may update these terms. When we do, the app asks you to read and agree again. These terms follow the laws of the Kingdom of Cambodia.

Contact

Chat with us in the app, or email info@kongthean.com.
