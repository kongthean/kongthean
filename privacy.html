Privacy Policy

Thean · kongthean.com · Last updated 15 November 2026

Thean is a Cambodian travel team that shares places, culture and travel tips on kongthean.com and on our social media pages. This page explains what information we handle and why.

What we collect

  • Chat and calls: when you chat or call our team from the website, we keep the messages, your chosen name, and the call details (time, length, and a recording only if one is made), so our team can answer you.
  • Feedback and forms: anything you choose to send us, such as a rating, a message or an email address.
  • Basic settings on your device: your language and theme choice, saved in your own browser.

Our Facebook page tools

Our team uses a private tool (an app called "Thean Post") to publish posts to our own Facebook Page and to show our Page's follower count and latest posts on this website. The tool only acts on our own Page. It does not collect information about website visitors or Facebook users.

Thean Post (posting app) accounts

If you create an account in our posting app, we keep your name, email, phone, Facebook profile link, your posting history (time, places, source link and caption), payment receipts and plans, and your chats with our team. Your Facebook, Instagram and Telegram keys stay only on your own phone. We use this information to run the app, check payments and keep it safe. If the Terms of use are broken, we may use it and share it with the competent authorities under the laws of the Kingdom of Cambodia.

How we use information

Only to reply to you, to improve our service, and to run this website. We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising.

Where it is stored

Information is stored with trusted services that run this website, such as Google Firebase, Cloudinary and GitHub Pages.

Deleting your information

To ask us to delete your chat, call or other information, email info@kongthean.com or send us a message in the website chat. We will delete it within 30 days.

Contact

Email: info@kongthean.com
